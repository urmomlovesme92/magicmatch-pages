Account Deletion

Delete Your MagicMatch Dating Account

You can request deletion of your MagicMatch Dating account and associated personal data.

Request Deletion In The App

  1. Open MagicMatch Dating.
  2. Sign in to the account you want deleted.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Choose Delete Account.
  5. Follow the prompts to submit your deletion request.

Request Deletion By Email

You can also email akhicks92@gmail.com with the subject line MagicMatch Dating Account Deletion Request.

Please include the email address connected to your MagicMatch Dating account.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile data, including profile text, preferences, quiz answers, and profile media where technically available.
  • Non-essential match, plan, and chat records associated with the account.
  • Non-essential location and planning records associated with the account.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment records, dispute resolution, platform obligations, or abuse prevention. Examples can include purchase records handled by Apple or Google, moderation reports, block/report records, legal records, and security logs.

Timing

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required or permitted by law, safety review, dispute handling, or platform requirements.
