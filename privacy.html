MagicMatch Dating is a dating, friendship, and social planning app for adults who want to meet around shared theme-park interests.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information, including email address and authentication details.
  • Profile information, including photos, bio, preferences, guided answers, quizzes, interests, and intent.
  • Optional profile media, including short videos and voice notes.
  • Chat messages, call session metadata, date plans, group plans, and post-date feedback you choose to submit.
  • Emergency contacts you choose to add for trusted-contact alerts.
  • Verification intake information, such as selected verification type, waiver acknowledgement, status, and provider handoff metadata.
  • Support requests, reports, blocks, moderation reports, and safety feedback.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Approximate or precise location when you grant permission for in-park mode, route guidance, meetup planning, or emergency location sharing.
  • Device, app, diagnostics, and usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Subscription and entitlement information from app stores and subscription providers.

Sensitive Information

Dating safety features may involve verification status, driver-license waiver acknowledgement, emergency contacts, safety reports, and optional location sharing. MagicMatch Dating is designed to store status and operational records, not raw background-check reports or raw identity documents after provider handoff.

How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain user accounts and profiles.
  • Provide matching, chat, voice/video call rooms, date planning, and group planning features.
  • Suggest park-aware date ideas, routes, crowd estimates, meetup windows, and official handoff links.
  • Support verification, waiver, trust badge, reporting, blocking, and moderation workflows.
  • Send trusted-contact emergency text drafts when a user chooses to announce an emergency.
  • Process purchases, restore subscriptions, and manage entitlements.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account deletion requests.
  • Detect abuse, enforce safety rules, comply with law, and improve app reliability.

How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help operate MagicMatch Dating, including:

  • Authentication, database, storage, and backend infrastructure providers.
  • Subscription, purchase, and entitlement providers, including Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.
  • Verification or background-check providers when you choose a verification path.
  • Map, routing, communications, support, moderation, analytics, and crash-reporting providers.
  • Official park, booking, ticketing, hotel, accessibility, or event websites only when you choose to open an external handoff link.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the public, to investigate abuse, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Location and Emergency Sharing

Location is optional. If granted, MagicMatch Dating may use location for in-park mode, AR-style meetup guidance, date routes, crowd context, and trusted-contact emergency alerts. Emergency location sharing happens when the user chooses to announce an emergency.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain records, respond to requests, enforce agreements, comply with law, or protect safety.

  • Future plans and location-related planning records may expire or be deleted after their operational need ends.
  • Blocked, reported, moderation, or safety records may be retained when needed to protect users and enforce rules.
  • Payment card details are handled by Apple, Google, and their payment systems, not stored directly by MagicMatch Dating.

You may request deletion through the in-app deletion flow or the public Account Deletion page.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location, camera, microphone, and photo permissions in device settings.
  • You can choose whether to verify, match, chat, plan, share a date plan, or use in-park mode.
  • You can block or report users inside the app.
  • You can cancel subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play settings.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Children

MagicMatch Dating is intended for adults only and is not directed to children or minors. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
