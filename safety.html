MagicMatch Dating Safety Guidelines

Safety guidance for matching, chatting, calling, planning, and meeting at theme parks.

Core Safety Rules

  • Meet in public places and stay inside well-trafficked areas.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Use in-app chat and call rooms before sharing a phone number.
  • Do not share financial information, passwords, codes, or private documents with matches.
  • Leave immediately and contact park security or emergency services if something feels unsafe.

MagicMatch Dating Safety Tools

  • Mutual match required before chat.
  • Consent-based plan visibility.
  • Optional verification and trust badge paths.
  • Blocking and reporting.
  • Trusted-contact emergency text drafts with optional one-time location sharing.
  • Public park meetup and date planning tools.

Emergency Notice

MagicMatch Dating does not replace 911, local emergency services, park security, or law enforcement. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services and contact nearby park staff.
