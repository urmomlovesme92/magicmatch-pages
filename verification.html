MagicMatch Dating Verification Info

How verification, waivers, trust badges, and background-check handoffs are intended to work.

Verification Paths

MagicMatch Dating may support one or more optional verification paths, including:

  • CrimShield or another third-party background-check provider handoff.
  • FBI clearance card review or validation path where available and appropriate.
  • Driver-license waiver acknowledgement for users who choose to chat without completing a background-check path.

What MagicMatch Stores

MagicMatch Dating is designed to store verification status, waiver acknowledgement, provider handoff metadata, timestamps, and trust-badge eligibility. It is not designed to store raw background-check reports or raw identity documents inside the app database after provider handoff.

Verification Limits

Verification can reduce risk, but it cannot guarantee that any person is safe, truthful, compatible, or free from future misconduct. Always use personal judgment, public meeting places, and the safety tools available in the app.
